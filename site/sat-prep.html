SAT Preparation — Latham, NY

Preparation built around the student.

ABLE's SAT preparation begins with a simple principle: students improve differently. A strategy that works beautifully for one student may not address what is holding another student back.

How it works

Find what the student needs — and teach from there

Rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all curriculum, we look carefully at each student's performance, learning patterns, strengths, and areas for growth. Instruction is then adjusted accordingly.

Students may receive support in:

  • SAT Reading and Writing
  • SAT Math
  • Test-taking strategies
  • Pacing and time management
  • Targeted skill development
  • Practice and performance analysis
  • Individualized feedback
  • Study habits and accountability

Common questions

SAT prep FAQ

When do SAT prep classes run?

Our SAT prep classes run during the summer only, every year — timed for the August SAT test date.

How many students are in each class?

We cap each summer class at 15 students. Once we're full, we don't accept more — small groups are how every student gets our full attention.

When does registration open?

Registration opens in January and spots are first come, first served. They fill up every year, so we recommend reaching out early.

How much does SAT prep cost?

Our instructor goes over pricing with you directly. Fill out the inquiry form below or text us at (518) 390-7346 to get started.

Is SAT prep in person or online?

Both. Students can join in person in the Capital Region or online.
