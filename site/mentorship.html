High School Mentorship — Latham, NY

Preparing for more than an application.

Some of the most meaningful work we do happens long before a college application is submitted.

What mentorship looks like

The student — not a checklist — is at the center

High school students are still discovering who they are. They may be deciding what interests them, learning how to manage demanding schedules, developing study habits, exploring possible careers, taking on leadership roles, or simply figuring out what they want their high school experience to look like.

ABLE provides individualized mentorship to help students navigate these years more intentionally.

Conversations may involve

  • Academic goals
  • Extracurricular interests
  • Organization & time management
  • Motivation & accountability
  • Summer opportunities
  • Leadership development
  • Preparation for future college planning
Why it matters

Students need more than information

They need someone who knows them well enough to recognize when they are capable of more, help them regain direction when they become overwhelmed, and hold them accountable — while still allowing them to take ownership of their work.

Confidence

Students learn to recognize their own strengths and trust their abilities.

Independence

Over time, students need less direction from us — not more.

Direction

Intentional choices about classes, activities, and summers — starting early.
