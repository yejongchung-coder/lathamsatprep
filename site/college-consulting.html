College essay coaching

Helping students find their own voice

The strongest college essays should sound like the student who wrote them.

ABLE helps students move from an initial idea through brainstorming, reflection, drafting, revision, and final refinement — while preserving the student's authentic voice.

We do not believe in manufacturing stories simply because they sound impressive. Instead, students are encouraged to think more deeply about their experiences and discover what those experiences reveal about who they are.

Our role is to teach students how to communicate those ideas with greater clarity, depth, and purpose.

College consulting FAQ

What does college admissions consulting include?

Individualized guidance through the whole process: building a college list, application planning, essay coaching, and staying on top of deadlines — all tailored to the student.

Do you help with college essays?

Yes. Our college essay coaching covers the personal statement and supplemental essays — finding the student's real story and shaping it into essays that stand out.

Is college consulting in person or online?

Both. Sessions are available in person and online, so Capital Region families can work with us either way.
