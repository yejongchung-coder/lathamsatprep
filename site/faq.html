Common questions

SAT prep FAQ

When do SAT prep classes run?

Our SAT prep classes run during the summer only, every year — timed for the August SAT test date.

How many students are in each class?

We cap each summer class at 15 students. Once we're full, we don't accept more — small groups are how every student gets our full attention.

When does registration open?

Registration opens in January and spots are first come, first served. They fill up every year, so we recommend reaching out early.

How much does SAT prep cost?

Our instructor goes over pricing with you directly. Fill out the inquiry form below or text us at (518) 738-9750 to get started.

Is SAT prep in person or online?

Both. Students can join in person in the Capital Region or online.

College consulting FAQ

What does college admissions consulting include?

Individualized guidance through the whole process: building a college list, application planning, essay coaching, and staying on top of deadlines — all tailored to the student.

Do you help with college essays?

Yes. Our college essay coaching covers the personal statement and supplemental essays — finding the student's real story and shaping it into essays that stand out.

Is college consulting in person or online?

Both. Sessions are available in person and online, so Capital Region families can work with us either way.
