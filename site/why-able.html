Why families choose ABLE

No two students should receive the same roadmap.

Every student comes to us with a different academic background, learning style, personality, set of strengths, challenges, goals, and vision for the future. Our programs are intentionally designed around the individual student — rather than asking students to fit into a predetermined program.

Led by Dr. Ceecee Chung

Experience in education

ABLE combines years of experience in education, psychology, SAT instruction, and college consulting to provide students with highly individualized guidance during some of the most important years of their academic lives.

That experience brings an important perspective: academic performance is only one part of understanding a student. Effective teaching requires knowing when to challenge, when to explain something differently, when to provide structure, and when to encourage a student to take greater ownership.

Our goal extends beyond a test score or a college acceptance. We want students to understand themselves as learners, recognize their strengths, develop confidence in their abilities, and become increasingly independent as they prepare for college and beyond.

Individualized by design

Individualized by design

Personalization is not an add-on at ABLE. It is the foundation of our work. We take time to understand how each student learns, communicates, responds to feedback, and approaches challenges. That understanding informs the support we provide.

Teaching, not just test prep

We want students to understand why an answer works — not simply memorize a shortcut. ABLE emphasizes foundational skills, reasoning, strategy, and thoughtful practice so students become more capable and confident test-takers.

Mentorship, not just consulting

Students often need more than information. They need someone who knows them well enough to recognize when they are capable of more, help them regain direction when they become overwhelmed, and hold them accountable while still allowing them to take ownership of their work.

A relationship with the student

Students are not simply names on a roster or application files to complete. We get to know them over time — their personalities, aspirations, strengths, frustrations, and growth. That relationship allows our guidance to become increasingly individualized as we work together.
